# TCPR Brand Theme Design

## Direction

Use the approved clean corporate style. The TCPR wide logo appears on a white header and on the login card. Brand navy `#333464` is the primary color for navigation, text links, and primary actions. Brand teal `#00A99C` is the accent for focus, highlights, and successful states.

## Layout

- Desktop header: wide logo at the left; navigation and account controls at the right.
- Mobile header: logo remains readable above or beside wrapped navigation.
- Login: centered branded card with the logo above the title.
- Pages: subtle navy/teal-tinted background, white cards, accessible contrast.

## Scope

Only visual presentation changes. Authentication, ticket workflow, data, and routes remain unchanged.

## Verification

An automated brand contract test checks the two exact colors and logo usage. Type checking, production build, and browser review cover regressions and responsive appearance.
